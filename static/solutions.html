Solutions

From walk-out to five stars.

Starling is the operating loop: ask for the review, capture the truth, reply in public or private, keep listings honest.

1. Ask

Log a visit. Automation sends a live review link by email or SMS when delivery is configured. Until then, the /r/ link still works from the dashboard.

2. Capture

Happy ratings publish. Tough ones open an inbox ticket so they never hit Google first.

3. Reply

Grok drafts; you send. Same inbox for chat, forms, and texts.

Reputation

Presence
